ABOUT / METHODOLOGY — TRACE

Sources: spotify_history.csv (149,860 plays) + Daily Household Transactions.csv (2,461 entries). Augmented India dataset NOT used. No PII-shaped fields. No movies/photos/messages/searches/places invented.

IST: Spotify timestamps converted UTC→IST +05:30 because ledger contains INR transactions and life records are Indian. ~30% plays midnight–5AM measured after IST conversion. No psychological claim.

Aggregation: Precomputed, not raw render. 45,721 plays 2015–2018. 632 days contain both music + ledger. Per-year top artist: The Beatles lead most years, 2015 different profile. Average logged salary rises across period. ₹1,500 Diwali gift recurs 2015 and 2016.

Connections: same date, same month, same year, nearby music timestamp (±90m), same category — labeled on every connection.

Design: Cinematic dark — investigative journalism + premium editorial + data storytelling + evidence discovery. Near-black depth with gradients/shadows. Huge editorial headings + monospace data. Warm off-white floating paper receipts. Restrained multi-color accents for meaning. Simple top nav. 1–2s opening. Subtle custom cursor (desktop). Fully keyboard navigable.
